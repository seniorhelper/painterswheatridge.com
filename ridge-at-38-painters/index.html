Two jobs on one street

The 38th Avenue corridor is where Wheat Ridge's commercial frontage and its residential core meet, often within a hundred feet. A storefront and the bungalow behind it need completely different scheduling even though they share a block.

Storefronts and small business

Almost everything along here is owner-operated, which means a lost day of trade is a real cost rather than a line item. Interior work happens evenings and overnight so you open on time. Exterior work is phased and contained, because the sidewalk is a few feet from the wall and there are people on it.

A good share of this frontage is genuinely old masonry. That generally needs a breathable coating rather than a standard exterior paint, and buildings predating 1978 bring the same lead-safe requirements a house does. Both are worth knowing before you collect bids.

The residential blocks behind

Streets like those around Olinger and the blocks north and south of 38th are compact, close-set and almost entirely pre-1978. Three things follow:

  • Containment matters more than equipment. Your neighbor's car, deck and garden are close enough to need real protection.
  • Lead-safe practices are the default. Certified handling, containment and verified cleanup when painted surfaces are disturbed.
  • Parking and staging are planned. On a street with no driveway to spare, where the truck sits is worked out before the first morning rather than at 7am.

Why these are efficient projects

Compact post-war homes with straightforward access are often a three-day exterior rather than a week. The preparation still has to be real, but the square footage is modest and the work moves predictably once it starts.

Questions

Ridge at 38 and Olinger questions

Can you paint my storefront without closing?

Yes. Interior work is scheduled evenings and overnight, and exterior work is phased with containment appropriate to a busy pedestrian sidewalk.

My building is old masonry. Does that change the coating?

Usually yes. Older masonry generally needs a breathable coating rather than a standard exterior paint, and buildings predating 1978 carry the same lead-safe requirements a house does.

Is parking a problem on these streets?

It is a planning item rather than a problem. Equipment placement and where the truck sits get sorted before the first morning.

How long does a house near 38th take?

Often three days for a complete exterior. Compact post-war homes with straightforward access are efficient projects when the preparation is scoped honestly.

Do you work with neighboring businesses on timing?

Where it helps, yes. On a shared frontage we will coordinate so two businesses are not disrupted in the same week.
