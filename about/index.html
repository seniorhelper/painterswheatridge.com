The short answer

Wheat Ridge Painters is the Wheat Ridge crew of Painter Hotline, a painting company headquartered in Denver with specialist crews for interiors, exteriors, cabinets, commercial work and decks. Here the crew concentrates on older and mid-century houses: lead-safe preparation before 1978, painted brick, plaster and hand texture, and trim that still has its original shape. Fully insured, more than 20 years of experience, and a 5-year workmanship warranty.

Who we are

Why a crew just for this city

Wheat Ridge filled in quickly in the 1950s as Jefferson County suburbs spread west from Denver, and the city did not incorporate until 1969. Walk any block between Kipling and Sheridan and you will see what that left behind: ranches with deep eaves, brick fronts that someone painted decades ago, steel casement windows, plaster in a few older houses and hand-troweled texture in many more.

Those houses reward patience. A crew that spends an extra morning scraping to a sound edge, testing what the old trim is coated with, or sealing a hallway before anyone sands will hand you a finish that lasts. A crew that skips those steps hands you a problem with a short fuse. We are often hired to fix other painters’ work, which is exactly why we would rather do it right the first time.

What you can expect from us

  • An estimate your way. Send photos for a virtual estimate, or have us walk the house with you. Both are free.
  • A written scope. Prep steps, primer, finish product and sheen for each surface, and the number of coats.
  • Lead-safe planning on older homes. If your house predates 1978 and the work disturbs paint, containment and cleanup are part of the plan, and you will receive the EPA Renovate Right pamphlet before work starts.
  • Any major brand. We paint with Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, Behr, Valspar, PPG and others, and we will tell you plainly which product suits each surface.
  • Five years of workmanship coverage. In writing. If our prep or application lets you down, we come back.

How a job runs

  1. Estimate. Photos or a walk-around, then a written number.
  2. Scheduling. Interiors book easily through winter; exterior dates fill from spring.
  3. Protection. Floors, furniture, plants and fixtures covered; work areas sealed on older homes.
  4. Prep. Washing, scraping, sanding, patching, priming. On an older house this is most of the job.
  5. Finish coats. As written on the estimate, nothing quietly skipped.
  6. Walkthrough. You point, we touch up, then the cleanup is checked before we leave.

Part of something bigger

Because we are one crew of a larger company, a Wheat Ridge homeowner gets a local team with the backing of specialist crews when a job needs them. The crews share one phone number, one warranty and one set of standards.

About us, briefly

Are you licensed?

Colorado does not issue a statewide painting license. What protects you is insurance, which we carry, and a detailed written scope you can hold us to.

Are you a referral service?

No. Painter Hotline is a painting company with its own specialist crews. The crew that estimates your Wheat Ridge home is the company doing the work.

Do you only paint older homes?

No, we paint any home or business in Wheat Ridge. Older and mid-century houses are simply where our extra care shows the most.
