What older interiors need

Plaster and hand texture

Some of the oldest houses here have plaster walls, and many more carry texture that a person applied with a trowel or brush rather than a spray rig. A patch that ignores how the original was made shows up every time a lamp is switched on. We look at what is on the wall, repair cracks properly, and rebuild texture by the same method before any primer goes on.

Original trim with history

Flat casings and plain baseboards from the 1950s are often buried under several coats, with the edges rounded off. Sanding them back toward their original shape, filling dings and caulking the gaps takes extra hours per room and gives you trim that looks like it was built that way.

Old oil enamel under the latex

Many older doors and trim were painted with oil-based enamel. Latex rolled straight over glossy oil can peel off in strips. A quick rubbing-alcohol test tells us what we are dealing with, and if it is oil, the surface is cleaned, dulled by sanding and sealed with a bonding primer first. Our guide explains the test: painting over oil-based trim.

Lead-safe when it counts

If the house was built before 1978 and the work disturbs old paint, the room is sealed, prep is done without open dry sanding, and cleanup happens before the room reopens. You will get the EPA Renovate Right pamphlet at least seven days ahead. See lead-safe painting.

Living at home while we paint

  • We work room by room so the rest of the house stays usable.
  • Bedrooms are usually finished and put back the same day.
  • Low-odor products are available from all major brands.
  • Windows can stay shut in January; good ventilation and the right product make that workable.

Quick answers

Older-home interior questions

Is winter a good time to paint the inside of my house?

Yes. Interior work does not depend on the weather, and winter dates are easier to get. 25% off the labor on painting projects booked by December 31, 2026. Paint and materials not included.

Can you fix cracked plaster before painting?

Yes. Cracks are opened, filled and reinforced where needed, and the texture is rebuilt to match before priming.

Why does new paint peel off my old trim?

Often because the trim was painted with oil-based enamel and latex went straight over it. The fix is cleaning, sanding off the gloss and a bonding primer.

Do I have to move out?

Usually not. We work room by room. If your home was built before 1978, plan to keep children and pregnant family members away from rooms being prepped.

Which paint brand will you use?

Whichever suits the surface and your preference. We paint with all major brands, including Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, Behr, Valspar and PPG.
