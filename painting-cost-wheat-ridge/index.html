The short answer

Published 2026 Front Range cost data puts exterior house painting at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface and interior painting at about $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot, with cabinet refinishing at $2,000 to $8,000 per kitchen. In Wheat Ridge the dominant cost factors are pre-1978 lead-safe requirements, the depth of layered old coatings that must be scraped back, and the linear feet of fascia and trim on long low rooflines.

Published ranges

Published 2026 Front Range cost data puts exterior house painting at roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface and interior painting at about $1.50 to $3.50 per square foot. Cabinet refinishing generally runs $2,000 to $8,000 per kitchen.

Those ranges are wide because condition drives price more than size does, and in Wheat Ridge condition means age.

Why the cheapest bid usually is not

Two bids on the same Wheat Ridge house. One is $6,000 and skips the wash, the scrape and the primer. One is $9,500 and does all three.

They look identical for about eighteen months. Then the cheap one starts lifting, because on a house with seventy years of layered coatings, nothing was holding the new paint to anything sound. The next painter has to remove the failure first, which costs more than painting bare siding would have.

You have spent $6,000, plus roughly $2,000 in removal, plus $9,500 for the real job. The $3,500 you saved cost about $8,000.

Where you can legitimately save

  • Book interiors November through March. Open calendar, no weather risk, better dates.
  • Phase the exterior. South and west walls fail first here. Do those now, plan the rest.
  • Do the whole exterior in one mobilization rather than splitting across two seasons and paying setup twice.
  • Clear rooms yourself before interior work.
  • Adjust the sprinklers. Free, and it stops a recurring failure at the base of the wall.
  • Coordinate with a neighbor. Two houses on one trip lowers both prices.

Four questions that explain any price gap

  1. How will you handle lead-safe requirements on a pre-1978 home?
  2. How much scraping is in this number, and what happens if there is more?
  3. Which surfaces get primer, and which primer specifically?
  4. What is the plan for the fascia and soffit?

Ask all four bidders in writing. The gap between their numbers usually explains itself immediately.

Questions

Cost questions

How much to paint a house in Wheat Ridge?

Published 2026 exterior data of roughly $1.55 to $4.10 per square foot of paintable surface puts most homes here in a four-figure range. Paintable surface is not floor area, and on a ranch the trim carries more of the cost than people expect.

Why is my quote higher than my friend's in a newer suburb?

Three reasons specific to older housing: legally required lead-safe practices on pre-1978 homes, far more scraping because of layered coatings, and more linear feet of fascia and soffit on a long low roofline.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Free in person or from photos, with no obligation.

When is payment due?

A deposit reserves your dates and the balance is due at completion, after you have walked the work and signed off. Terms are printed on the estimate.

Can I phase the work to spread the cost?

Yes, and it is sensible. South and west elevations take the most ultraviolet here and fail first, so do those now and plan the rest for next season.

Does lead-safe work really add cost?

Yes. Containment, defined work practices and verified cleanup all take time. It is a federal requirement on pre-1978 housing rather than an upsell, and that is most of Wheat Ridge.
